About

Pomofarm is a quiet pomodoro timer. While you focus, an egg warms, hatches and grows into a chicken, a duck or a goose.

  • Every finished focus session helps your pet grow. After the number of sessions you picked, it grows up and moves to your farm.
  • Take a break between sessions. Your pet pecks around and, after a few seconds, curls up for a nap. Tap it to wake it.
  • Give up in the middle of a session and the egg goes cold.
  • Open My farm to feed your pets and see today's focus time.
