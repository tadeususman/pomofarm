# Pomofarm

A minimalist pomodoro focus timer where a chick, duckling or gosling grows while you focus.

- Three side-view characters (chicken, duck, goose) with their own sounds that grow deeper as they grow
- Wings move while walking, adults bow smoothly to peck worms, growth stages cross-fade
- Two-color look (ink + paper), light / dark / auto theme
- Hatch an egg, grow it over a configurable number of focus sessions, then let it live on your farm
- Tap the farm to scatter feed, pets also drink at the trough
- Mini window (Document Picture-in-Picture) in Chrome / Edge

Single self-contained file: open `index.html` in a browser.
