Real recordings used instead of the synthesized animal sounds:

- `chick.mp3`, `chick2.mp3`, `chick3.mp3`, `chick4.mp3` - four chick peeps (a random one plays each time)
- `duck.mp3` - a single quack
- `goose.mp3`, `goose2.mp3`, `goose3.mp3`, `goose4.mp3` - four honks

Extra variants are picked up automatically (`duck2.mp3`, `duck3.mp3` ... up to 6). `.ogg`, `.wav` and `.m4a` also work.
Use short clips (under ~1.5 s) with little silence at the start. The app plays them higher and quieter for babies and lower and louder for adults.

Chick peeps cut from "short chick sound" by nikin, the quack from "duck quack" by iedurodrigues, and the honks from "goose 2" (freesound_community), all from Pixabay.
