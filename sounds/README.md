Drop real recordings here to replace the synthesized animal sounds:

- `chick.mp3` (a single short cluck / peep)
- `duck.mp3` (a single quack)
- `goose.mp3` (a single honk)

`.ogg`, `.wav` and `.m4a` also work. Use short clips (under ~1.5 s) with little silence at the start.
The app plays them higher and quieter for babies and lower and louder for adults. Make sure the files are free to use.
